Document: Examination Regulations
Section: Assessments and results

Students must register for examinations before the published registration deadline.
The examination office publishes the examination timetable and seating arrangements.
The controller of examinations oversees examination administration and result publication.
Students who believe a result contains an error may submit a revaluation request.
Medical leave may be considered when a student submits valid supporting documentation for an examination absence.
The examination office coordinates with departments to resolve timetable conflicts.
