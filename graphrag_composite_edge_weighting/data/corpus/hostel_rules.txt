Document: Hostel Rules
Section: Residence and facilities

Hostel residents must follow the hostel regulations published by the warden.
The hostel mess provides meals to hostel residents according to the mess schedule.
The warden handles hostel complaints and coordinates maintenance requests.
Hostel residents may apply for medical leave through the same student leave process.
Residents must keep their rooms clean and report urgent safety issues to the hostel office.
The hostel office maintains records of room allocation and resident contact details.
