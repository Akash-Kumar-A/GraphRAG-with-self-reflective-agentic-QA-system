Document: Medical Leave Policy
Section: Student medical leave

A student requesting medical leave must submit a medical certificate issued by a registered medical practitioner.
The Dean of Academic Affairs reviews and approves medical leave applications.
Students should notify their department office when medical leave extends beyond three working days.
Medical leave does not automatically extend assignment deadlines; students must contact the course instructor.
A medical certificate is required as evidence for a medical leave request.
The department office records approved student leave and informs the relevant course instructors.
