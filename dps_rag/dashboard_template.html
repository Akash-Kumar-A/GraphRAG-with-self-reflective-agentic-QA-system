Spending privacy only where the answer needs it

DPS layer of the project: differentially private RAG (DPVoteRAG and DPSparseVoteRAG, Koga et al. 2025) over a confidential student-services corpus, with the PSG Tech public corpus as the public language model.

Watch the budget being spent

Each answer is generated token by token. Teal tokens come from the public model and cost nothing; amber tokens were voted by 50 voters who each saw different confidential records, and each one spends privacy budget.

Utility under a privacy budget

Slot accuracy: how many of the four confidential facts (month, reason, outcome, reviewer) the answer states correctly, averaged over 12 questions and 10 seeds. DP rows use the best (m, εtoken) for each εtotal.

Hyperparameters

Mean slot accuracy by number of voters m and per-token budget εtoken.

Privacy accountant

Maximum number of private tokens (cmax) allowed by composition, with δtotal = 10⁻⁴ and δtoken = 10⁻⁵, and the ε actually consumed by DPSparseVoteRAG at m = 50.

Does it leak? Membership inference

Attacker queries with a record's own opening ("Student … submitted a … request in the month of") and scores the answer's overlap with the true continuation. AUC 0.5 means the attacker cannot tell members from non-members. 144 members and 144 non-members, bootstrap 95% interval.
